Pack, Trunk & Side Deck mechanics only apply to YGO cards.

Meaning if you get colorless cards or curses, or cards from other characters they will behave as normal, having no interactions with the duelists packs or trunk / side deck system.

So everyone starts with 10 cards,

The silent starts with 12 cards because of pacing (more draw turn 1, and lots of draw / discard cards & cycling in their deck).

Our class has effectively everything exhaust (graveyard or shadow realm) and is pacing equivalent to silent due to field persistence.


Our class has a Trunk & Side Deck. They share one relic, you can click it to toggle the GUI visibility.

The Trunk & Side Deck GUI has 3 pages, the open page stays selected between visibility toggles.

The first page is viewing only Side Deck cards and being able to select any number of them 
and hit confirm to move those cards to the Trunk.

The second page is viewing only Trunk cards and being able to select any number of them 
and hit confirm to move those cards to the Side Deck.

The third page will be a left-right split-screen view with a selection grid on the left half pile 
of your screen being able to select any number of them and hit confirm to move those cards to the pile on the right half.
There is a swap icon to swap between the Side deck being on the left or the Trunk being on the left.

To clarify, the third page has a "selection" grid for one pile on the left half of your screen, 
and a purely Viewing grid for the other pile on the right half of your screen.


The Trunk & Side Deck are storage for cards you obtain from card packs 
that you can consider for your main deck at a camp fire later on.

fusion monster cards CAN and WILL go into the Side Deck and Trunk, 
But when they are put in the normal Deck they instead go into the Extra Deck.

Due to these reasons, I think the starting deck should be in the range of 16-22 cards

Now back to the consideration of deck sizes then

I think every time you take a card pack you need to increase your Minimum Deck Size by 1. 

When you remove a card you lower your Minimum Deck Size by 1, in addition to being able to get rid of a card you don't want

Now onto how the Side deck & Trunk interact with this, and why I said "minimum"

For now lets say a starting deck and minimum deck size of 16? (starting with 3 basic strikes & defends.)

Before the player gets to select a Neows Blessing they have to form their starting deck.

Your starting deck will be formed by giving the player a selection grid of 18 random cards with the "starter" tag. 
(three rows of 6)

They must select and add at least 10 of them to their deck. 

At campfires as always available  Deck Edit bonus actions ->
You get 8? ygo deck edit actions, you can use each action for one of three things (only upon ygo cards),

-> replacing a card in your deck (deck size unchanged)
-> adding a card to your deck (increasing it size, max size 2x minimum up to 99 for sanity sake), 
-> removing a card from your deck (decreasing its size, but not below your minimum deck size)

So you can decide to expand if you think you'll have problems in a prolonged fight. 
Or shrink out bloat or sub in cards you had side-decked (because you wanted more consistency before you added them) 
And this way its not infinite versatility or making campfires less valuable 
or changing the deckbuilding pacing too crazy much from the base game
