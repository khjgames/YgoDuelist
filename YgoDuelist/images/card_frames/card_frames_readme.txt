Place YGO card frame (art roller) backgrounds here. Filenames must match:

  ygo_spell.png         - Spell cards (e.g. Pot of Greed)
  ygo_trap.png          - Trap cards
  ygo_monster.png       - Normal monsters
  ygo_effect_monster.png - Effect monsters
  ygo_fusion_monster.png - Fusion monsters
  ygo_ritual_monster.png - Ritual monsters

Only add the types you use. Cards implementing IYgoCard with a given YgoCardType will use the matching image.
