All modes for monster cards cost unique different amounts of energy.
Monster Effect activations will have a specified energy cost.
Summoning Monsters in Attack or using Command Attack will use their Attack Energy cost, and Summoning Monsters in Defense or using Command Defense will use their Defense Energy Cost. 

There is a logical formula for defining the base attack energy and base defense energy costs based upon the monsters default un-changed level and default un-upgraded un-changed attack and defense values.

How you should scale and calculate the energy cost for attacks and defense based upon an individual default base level & attack or defense stat off "Z" is as follows.


If Z is -1 or ? cost 1.   Else: 

For Non Ritual Non Fusion monsters of level 4 and below ->
Z <= 3, cost 0.
Z >= 4 && Z <= 11, cost 1.
Z >= 12 && Z <= 20, cost 2.
Z >= 21 cost 3.
For Ritual or Fusion monsters of level 4 and below ->
Z <= 6, cost 0.
Z >= 7 && Z <= 14, cost 1.
Z >= 15 && Z <= 23, cost 2.
Z >= 24 cost 3.
For monsters of level 5 & 6 ->
Z <= 8, cost 0.
Z >= 9 && Z <= 16, cost 1.
Z >= 17 && Z <= 25, cost 2.
Z >= 26 cost 3.
For monsters of level 7 & 8 ->
Z <= 10, cost 0.
Z >= 11 && Z <= 17, cost 1.
Z >= 18 && Z <= 30, cost 2.
Z >= 31 cost 3.
For monsters of level 9&10 ->
Z <= 11, cost 0.
Z >= 12 && Z <= 25, cost 1.
Z >= 26 && Z <= 45, cost 2.
Z >= 46 cost 3.
For monsters of level 11+ ->
Z <= 12, cost 0.
Z >= 13 && Z <= 29, cost 1.
Z >= 30 && Z <= 60, cost 2.
Z >= 61 cost 3.
